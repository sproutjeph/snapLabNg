# SnapLab NG

A student snaps the WAEC practical in their exercise book. The apparatus rises out of the ink as a working 3D lab. An AI examiner then marks the student's own handwriting. Built solo for the Meta Global AI Developer Hackathon (Indie track, Oct 26 – Nov 4, 2026).

## Built before kickoff

The hackathon kicked off on **Mon Oct 26, 2026 at 12:00 PM ET (17:00 WAT, 16:00 UTC)**. To be clear about what was made during the event:

- **Before Oct 26:** research, planning and design (`docs/`, dated Oct 21–25). The repository's first commit is on Oct 26.
- **Oct 26, 11:05–13:56 WAT (a few hours before kickoff):** the first 13 commits, up to and including `aef17f2` and tagged [`pre-kickoff`](../../tree/pre-kickoff). They contain:
  - the planning docs, agent rules and workspace setup;
  - the 3D desk and lined page, and a screenshot script (#1);
  - the procedural metre bridge apparatus (#3) and its link to the engine (#7);
  - the shared data schemas (#5);
  - the metre bridge circuit engine (#6);
  - the photo capture flow, page flattening and ink extraction (#8, #10);
  - a Meta API check script with its first recorded responses (#11);
  - the "Show the AI" drawer UI (#12).
- **Everything after the `pre-kickoff` tag** was built during the hackathon. The commit dates on `main` are the record. To see it all: compare `pre-kickoff...main`.

The demo video presents only work built after kickoff as new.
